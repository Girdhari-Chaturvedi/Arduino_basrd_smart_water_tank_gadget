# Arduino_basrd_smart_water_tank_gadget
An Arduino Uno-based system that automates water tanks. Using three level sensors, it tracks water at 10%, 50%, and 90% with Red, Blue, and Green LEDs. A buzzer gives a continuous beep at 10% and 4 beeps at 90%. A 2-channel relay automatically switches the water pump ON at 10% and OFF at 90% to prevent dry running and water overflow.
